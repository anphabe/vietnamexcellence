Spartronics Vietnam, a leading electronics company in Vietnam, has been operating since 2005 in a 27,000m2 factory situated in Nam Tan Uyen Industrial Park, Binh Duong. The factory has achieved the LEED GOLD certification from the U.S. Green Building Council (USGBC) for its environmental sustainability.

Mr. Dung Tran is a visionary leader who is passionate about human development and Vietnam's prosperity. He chose to leave behind his opportunities in the US and return to his homeland to pursue his dreams.
As the General Director of Spartronics Vietnam since 2014, Mr. Dung Tran has led the company to achieve remarkable growth and revenue from $10 million to $100 million. He has also helped Spartronics Vietnam maintain its leading position in the group for many years.

To obtain these achievements, Mr. Dung Tran has to overcome many challenges, especially those that involved people.

Retaining talents and ensuring the quality of workforce when moving the factory away from the center

In 2021, Spartronics Vietnam has made decision to move its factory to Binh Duong to accomodate the growing scope of production. Because most of Spartronics’s employees live in Ho Chi Minh City, this will affect their decision to engage and stay.

To encourage employees to continue to engage and commit, Mr. Dung Tran and his team execute several supporting strategies and ideas:

- Shortening on-site working time
- Arranging alternative Saturdays off
- Supporting employees to find accommodation
- Providing travel allowance for those unable to relocate

He also attaches great importance to employees’ health and well-being. During the new factory construction period, Mr. Dung Tran stayed up late with construction team to inspect and ensure that the completion venue is not only a professional work environment but also include outstanding utilities for his employees, rivaling even world's leading companies like Microsoft. 


However, the surge in revenue has posed a dilemma on how to attract and develop human resources in time for the company.

Mr. Dung Tran, who has a passion for people development, has responsed with strategies that focused on internal talent development, building and developing a professional training system.

Believing that skillful workers are the key to productivity and that each employee is a teacher and can help guide others to learn and grow, he establised a core team in charge of essential courses for employees, especially in electronics field, thus creating the main force to help reach the 100 million dollar vision.

Especially, Mr. Dung Tran is the one who builds career development orientation for employee as soon as they join in Spartronics Vietnam, directly involves in training and guidance of both soft skills and job expertise.

Furthermore, Talkshow "Leadership Counseling Program" is an opportunity to bridge the gap between leaders and employees. He does not hesitate to spend his precious time to advise and help them when they have troubles in work as well as in life.

He pays great attention to building vision and executing strategy, as well as building culture identity, raising trust, and developing and attracting talents. These are what motivate Spartronics Vietnam to maintain Top 1 position in the group for many years.
