Saint-Gobain is a global leader in construction materials and solutions, founded in France in 1665. Saint-Gobain Vietnam is a subsidiary of Saint-Gobain Group, offering a wide range of products and services for the construction industry under various brands, such as VINH TUONG, VINH TUONG-Gyproc, DURAflex, Weber, ISOVER, PAM, Vetrotech, Norton, Eurokera, ADFORS, SEKURIT12. Saint-Gobain Vietnam is committed to improving the quality of living and working spaces by providing innovative and sustainable solutions for life and the environment. Saint-Gobain’s mission is to make the world a better home.

COVID has put the businesses up against numerous challenges, following by the great resignation spreading across the market. By understanding and expressing CARE to every employee and their family, Saint-Gobain Vietnam has steadily been improving and innovating well-being activities to build up an open, safe, and happy working environment for employees. Health is highly prioritized at Saint-Gobain Vietnam, many policies and initiatives have been put in place early during the Covid-19 pandemic to ensure the health of employees, business & partners. Afterwards, CARE Culture has been consistently driven in all activities.

At Saint-Gobain, TEC CULTURE is a core value with three key pillars that fosters a professional environment: TRUST - EMPOWERMENT – COLLABORATION. This helps the company build a trusted high-performance team, be faster in actions, and be more agile to adapt to customers’ needs.
