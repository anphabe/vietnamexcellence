Lazada is the best-in-class eCommerce platform in Southeast Asia, with a strong presence in five countries: Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore and Vietnam. As a regional flagship platform of Alibaba Group, Lazada leverages the technology, infrastructure and payment capabilities of the global leader in online commerce. Talent Acquisition and Learning Development are 2 key pillars for cultivate a robust organization and incubate talents in Lazada.

TALENT ACQUISITION: ATTRACT ‘RIGHT’ TALENTS

Lazada strives to connect as many potential talents as possible by:

- Establish relationship with 30 universities
- Working with 10 community partners
- Spreading awareness on social media platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Tiktok)

Spreading Vietnamese Coffee Culture Internationally

Highlands Coffee takes immense pride in being the earliest Vietnamese F&B chain to expand internationally. With 53 stores across 22 provinces and cities in Philippines, Highlands Coffee has become a source of pride and nostalgia for the Vietnamese community abroad.

Featuring by unique store designs and a menu written prominently in Vietnamese, they have successfully created a comforting connection to their roots in every location even overseas.

Building a culture of entrepreneurship, empowerment, and autonomy, the leadership team empowers store managers as ‘hosts’ of their stores. This approach instills a deep sense of ownership among team members, making them active contributors to the company’s success. This spirit of solidarity and unity has helped Highlands Coffee overcome challenges, including those brought about by the COVID-19 pandemic.

Prioritizing Well-being and Fostering a Strong and Sustainable Highlands Community

The leadership team addresses and ensures the health and well-being of employees through comprehensive training programs, promoting healthy living and nurturing the mind. They believe that a HEALTHY and THANKFUL team forms the foundation of a strong and sustainable community.

Highlands Coffee extends its commitment beyond employees and customers to include their families, creating a network immersed in love and care.

Embracing the Journey: Highlands Coffee, A Brand for All

As Highlands Coffee continues its journey upholding a new message “Highlands Coffee® Is Ours”, the brand remains dedicated to its vision of bringing the best of Vietnam to the world while staying true to its roots. With its exceptional vision, unwavering commitment to Vietnamese culture, and a passionate community of members, Highlands Coffee is poised to make a lasting impact in the industry and serve as an inspiring example for brands worldwide.

In the words of David Thai - Founder & CEO of Highlands Coffee, "I had a dream, but it's no longer just my dream. I realized that this dream is bigger than me, bigger than all of us. TOGETHER, LET'S BRING THE BEST OF VIETNAM TO THE WORLD." This inspiring message completely encapsulates the spirit of Highlands Coffee® and its commitment to creating a vibrant community brand that celebrates Vietnamese heritage, fosters unity, and embraces the passion and creativity of its members.
