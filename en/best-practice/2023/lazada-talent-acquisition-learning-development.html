Lazada is the best-in-class eCommerce platform in Southeast Asia, with a strong presence in five countries: Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore and Vietnam. As a regional flagship platform of Alibaba Group, Lazada leverages the technology, infrastructure and payment capabilities of the global leader in online commerce. Lazada is proud to have a diverse and multicultural team of over 50 nationalities, speaking more than 100 languages. Talent Acquisition and Learning Development are 2 key pillars for cultivate a robust organization and incubate talents in Lazada.

TALENT ACQUISITION: ATTRACT ‘RIGHT’ TALENTS

Lazada strives to connect as many potential talents as possible by:

- Establish relationship with 30 universities
- Working with 10 community partners
- Spreading awareness on social media platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, Tiktok)

These initiatives are part of Lazada's career orientation journey, which highlights the opportunities and benefits of working in the ecommerce sector. 

Lazada values its employees and believes that they can help build the company’s influence to wider audience. As a result, “TALENTS KNOW GREAT TALENTS” was launched to encourage employee engagement and empower them to showcase their great experiences and opportunities at Lazada with their personal social channels. Activities that supported this goal and involved employees included Talent Ambassador, Lazrefer, and more.
