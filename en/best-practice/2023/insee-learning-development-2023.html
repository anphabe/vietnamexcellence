INSEE Vietnam is a member of Siam City Cement Public Company Limited (SCCC) - known as the leading construction material corporation in the region. The company has almost 1,000 employees working at 01 Plant, 04 Cement Grinding Stations, and the Head Office in Ho Chi Minh City.

Since its establishment in 1994, INSEE Vietnam has become one of the leading manufacturers of cement, construction mortar, Conwood and providing leading waste management services in the South of Vietnam – Ecocycle. With the philosophy of "Build for Life", INSEE is continuously creating a world where life is worth living.

Over the years, although it is a male-specific construction industry, INSEE has built a diverse, competent and passionate workforce

CHALLENGE

The Hon Chong plant is a vital part of INSEE's operation, as it affects the performance of the entire company. However, it is located in Kien Luong, Kien Giang province, a remote area that poses a challenge for attracting and retaining a skilled and reliable workforce.

"How to have a sustainable and competent workforce to ensure efficient operation in such distant location?"

SOLUTION

“Our solution is Enterprise based Vocational Education (EVE)”
Ms. Tran Hai Ninh, Human Resources Director of INSEE Vietnam.

INSEE has collaborated with Kien Giang College to offer an engineer training program called “Enterprise-based Vocational Education” (EVE). The goal is building a sustainable and competent workforce according to INSEE standard at technician level, developing capability for residents in the surrounding area to improve their skill and career opportunity, thus raise their living standard and subsequently, contribute to develop the area.

Holistic Program

Detailed program was developed for targeted future role

The Program has been implemented for 21 batches of EVE with good retention rate

The Program has significantly contributed to the technician workforce for INSEE and improved skill for local residents

“I very proud to continue this great traditional of local employees for our Business. We now have over 25% of our employees in Hon Chong plant from EVE and it’s our goal to make it over 50% in the near future. The continued presence of the female sisters in this community will only continue to help our company grow into the fully diverse company in Vietnam Cement Industry.” Mr. Fabio Cesconetto – Manufacturing Director

Over the time, INSEE will continue to move forward to promote key learning and bring excellent practices to drive business sustainability.
