VinaCapital is the only multi-disciplinary investment manager in Vietnam with 20 years of experience and $3.6 billion assets under management. They create value and share the opportunities and benefits of the country’s growth across every asset class. Their mission is to discover the best investment opportunities in Vietnam by leveraging extensive relationships, local investment knowledge, analytical capabilities, and financial expertise. The compassion for people and culture of VinaCapital’s senior leaders is evident in the way they lead the team, conduct the business and participate in philanthropy, remaining tirelessly committed to positively impacting the broader community in life and work.

People leadership philosophy: Nurturing an inclusive and rewarding workplace that empowers employees

Pillar 1: Leaders take great care of staff’s welfare 

Organized a series of Survival Training workshops, from understanding of the unpredictability of post Covid-19 time and other health concerns.

Cooperated with Life-work to build Employee Assistance Program which is 24/7 employee counselling and coaching support across all aspects of work and life.

Partnered with Procoach to organize various Wellness Workshops to help employees manage stress and improve their well-being.

Created an Employee Assistance Fund to provide staff with financial support regarding family tragedies; destruction of home/property by fire or other disasters; short-term disabilities where income is curtailed; and any other temporary hardships beyond the employee's control.

Pillar 2: Build a “Work Hard, Play Hard” family with active engagement of senior management

Pillar 3: Recognize good performance & Groom future leaders

Training and Scholarship policy

Senior management consistently offers training courses and Scholarship policy, even during difficult years, for staff at all levels to develop the necessary skills to achieve their work objectives.

Customized EQ assessment and training

for staff from manager level and above to help senior staff build stronger relationships and improve their overall job satisfaction as well as other team members' satisfaction.

Quarterly sharing sessions

Where staff can gain knowledge across departments and industries. By being a facilitator of these sessions, the presenting staff will also be trained with presentation and public speaking skills. Senior management will always be there to give feedback to the session.

Executive coaching program

To groom the next leadership team, senior management equips middle managers with customized coaching program to help them maximize their leadership potential and be ready to step up when the time comes.

Candy Gram Appreciation Campaign

This initiative, in the spirit of instilling even greater camaraderie within the company, allows employees to show appreciation to their beloved colleagues via written messages. They can wish good luck, spread some good cheers, or simply say thanks.

Pillar 4: Create opportunities to contribute back to society

HEALTHCARE, MEDICAL and EDUCATION PROGRAMS are 3 key focus areas for empowering women and alleviating poverty in Vietnam.
